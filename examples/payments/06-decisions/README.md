# Decisions

Append-only. One file per material decision, plus `decision-log.md` as the index.

A decision record exists to explain a choice to someone who was not in the room,
including yourself in a year. It carries the alternatives that were rejected and
why — without those it is an announcement, not a record.

When a decision is reversed, add a new record and mark the old one `superseded`
with a link to its replacement. Never edit a past decision into agreement with
the present.
