# Decision record: do not put AI in the H1, nav, or product name

## Decision ID

D-006

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Public surfaces do not use "AI", "artificial intelligence", or a model vendor's name in the H1, the navigation, or the product name. The models can exist in the product. They are not the pitch.

## Rationale

"AI reliability" is the category IT already bought (E-004, E-005). Using it is how we get the meeting we lose.
