# Goals

Approved goals only. A goal here has a number, a date and an owner; without all three it is an intention and belongs in a strategy document instead.

Period: 1 January 2026 – 31 December 2026.

## Current period

| Goal | Measure | Baseline (1 Jan 2026) | Target | By when | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qualified pipeline | Opportunities in HubSpot stage SQL or later | 9 | 40 | 2026-12-31 | Operations Lead |
| Closed-won | New logos, booked | 4 (2025 full year) | 12 | 2026-12-31 | Product Lead |
| Organic demo requests | HubSpot source = Organic / Direct / Referral | 4 / month (Dec 2025) | 30 / month | 2026-12-31 | Growth Lead |
| Delivery load | Parallel onboardings in flight | 2 | Stay ≤ 4 | ongoing | Delivery Lead |

Record the baseline at the moment the goal is set. A target without a baseline cannot be scored.
