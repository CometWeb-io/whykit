# Decision record: stay in discrete manufacturing through 2026

## Decision ID

D-008

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Automatica 2026 produced a line of process-industry visitors who were curious and unconvertible. E-012. Delivery has no package for them. Expanding would be a second product.

## Decision

Northline does not sell Pulse into process industry (chemicals, food, pharma continuous, oil and gas) as a 2026 motion. Inbound from those plants is disqualified politely.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Upside | Risk | Why rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| A "listen and learn" pilot | Maybe a wedge | Consumes a delivery slot we have four of | E-011 |
| Partner does the process version | Revenue without build | We still own support | Not this year |
