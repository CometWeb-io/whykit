# Decision record: ICP is ops, not IT

## Decision ID

D-002

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Early conversations drifted toward reliability-engineering and IT buyers, because that is who takes the meetings. Win/loss in H1 said otherwise.

> [!fact] Verified fact
> 7 of 9 lost deals in H1 2026 cited an existing IT-bought reliability tool as the reason. E-004.

Interviews said the artefact that would actually get opened was a shift list. E-002.

## Decision

The ideal customer is an operations or maintenance lead at a discrete plant. IT is a gate, not a buyer. Messaging, qualification and the website are aligned to that.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Upside | Risk | Why rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dual ICP, ops and IT | Larger top of funnel | Two products, two messages, we are 28 people | Capacity, and we lose the IT deals anyway |
| Sell through the incumbent platform | Distribution | We become a feature | Not our offer |

## Consequences

### Positive

- Qualification got simpler. See [[05-operations/qualification]].
- Homepage copy got shorter.

### Negative and trade-offs

- We will be invisible in category round-ups written for reliability engineers.
