# Decision record: HubSpot stays CRM of record

## Decision ID

D-007

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

HubSpot is the CRM of record for leads, accounts, opportunities and activities. This vault does not store pipeline state, and a personal spreadsheet does not either.

## Rationale

Two CRMs is how attribution dies. The vault holds why a stage definition is what it is — see [[05-operations/qualification]] — not the records themselves.
