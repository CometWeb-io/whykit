# Search and answers

## Intent we want

Queries a maintenance lead types at a desk, in German or English, about a machine that already exists:

- "vibration sensor for stamping press"
- "Schichtbeginn Maschinenliste"
- "condition monitoring ohne IT-Projekt"

## Intent we do not want

- "best AI predictive maintenance platform"
- Category round-ups aimed at reliability-engineering buyers. We are invisible there on purpose.

## Current measurement

> [!fact] Verified fact
> 38 queries with more than 10 impressions in the last 90 days. Source: Google Search Console, E-009. Unique visitors in August: 4,812, E-003. Organic demo requests in August: 6 of 11, E-010.

> [!hypothesis] Hypothesis
> Answer engines (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity) will cite the machine-list page if it is the clearest definition on the public web. Untested. We have no answer-engine citation log yet.

## Rules

- One canonical URL per topic. Do not write a blog post that restates [[01-strategy/positioning]] in softer language.
- Every public page names its evidence or does not make the claim.
- No comparison pages against named competitors until legal has a position. None exists today.
