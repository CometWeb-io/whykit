# Messaging

Derived from [[01-strategy/positioning]]. If a line cannot be traced to a fact or a decision, it does not ship.

## Home message

Tomorrow's machine list, before the shift starts.

## Proof points we may use

- The artefact is a list, not a dashboard. E-007.
- Onboarding is capped and productised. E-011, [[01-strategy/offer-architecture]].
- Sold to the person who owns the shift, not the person who owns the stack. [[06-decisions/d-002-icp-ops|D-002]].

## Proof points we may not use

- Any unattributed downtime percentage.
- "AI-powered" or model-vendor name-dropping. [[06-decisions/d-006-no-ai-in-h1|D-006]].
- Customer logos without a signed reference. We have four closed-won in 2025; two have declined public reference. Do not imply a roster we cannot show.
- Rankings, "leader", "fastest-growing".

## Voice

- Address the supervisor's morning, not the industry's transformation.
- German and English are both first-class. Do not ship Anglicisms in German outbound.
- Short sentences. No "unlock", "empower", "seamless", "next-generation".

## Alternative headlines considered

| Line | Why not |
|---|---|
| The AI reliability layer for mid-market manufacturing | Violates D-006; frames against a category we lose |
| See every machine | We do not, and "see" is dashboard language |
| Predictive maintenance, finally simple | Category cliché; "predictive maintenance" is what IT already bought |
