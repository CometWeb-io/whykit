# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to make the project better. Argue
about the work, not the person making it.

## Standards

Behavior that helps:

- Direct, specific, technical feedback.
- Saying "I was wrong" when you were.
- Assuming a confusing contribution is a misunderstanding before assuming malice.
- Accepting that a maintainer may say no to a good idea because of scope.

Behavior that does not:

- Personal attacks, harassment, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language or imagery; unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Demeaning someone for their experience level, background or first language.
  This project's contributors do not all write English natively, and precision
  matters here more than fluency.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **hello@cometweb.io**. Reports go to the maintainers at
CometWeb and stay confidential. We will respond within five working days.

Consequences scale with severity and repetition: a private word, a public
correction, a temporary block, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who break these
rules are held to them more strictly, not less.

If a report concerns a maintainer, say so in the report; it will be handled by
someone else.
