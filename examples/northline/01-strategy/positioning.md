# Positioning

## The sentence

Northline Pulse tells a discrete-manufacturing supervisor which machines will fail on the next shift, in a list they already know how to run.

That is the position. Everything public either supports it or is cut.

## Who we are for

Operations and maintenance leads at discrete plants, 200–2,000 employees, DACH first, with a CMMS already in place. Full profile: [[01-strategy/icp]].

## Who we are not for

Reliability-engineering organisations that already bought an IT-owned "AI reliability platform". We lose those deals on purpose — see E-004 and [[06-decisions/d-002-icp-ops|D-002]].

## The job to be done

> [!fact] Verified fact
> In 12 interviews in Q2, the artefact supervisors said they would actually open was a short list of machines for the next shift, not a dashboard of health scores. E-002, E-007.

The job is not "give me visibility". The job is "tell me what to do before the shift starts, in the same shape as the list I already write on paper."

## Competitive frame

Do not frame against "AI platforms". Frame against the paper list and the CMMS work-order queue. That is what Pulse replaces as a daily habit. Vendor A is a fictional example of a different category and buyer — [[07-research/competitors/vendor-a]].

> [!hypothesis] Hypothesis
> Plants that have already deployed an IT-owned vibration platform are not a lost cause if the supervisor still keeps a paper list. Untested. Do not put this in public copy.

## Category of one

We do not claim a new category in public. Internally: supervisor-grade condition monitoring. Externally: the product does the explaining.

> [!decision] Decision
> Do not put "AI" in the H1, the nav, or the product name. [[06-decisions/d-006-no-ai-in-h1|D-006]].
