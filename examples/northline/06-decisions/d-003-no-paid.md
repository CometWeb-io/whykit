# Decision record: no paid acquisition until organic baseline

## Decision ID

D-003

## Status

Accepted. Reaffirmed 2026-09-04.

## Context

The 2026 goal includes 30 organic demo requests a month. August produced 6. The temptation is to buy the rest.

## Decision

No paid search, paid social, or paid content syndication until organic demo requests hold 30 in a calendar month, or this decision is superseded.

## Rationale

Paid traffic would make it impossible to tell whether the position, the pages, and the ICP are working. We would be scoring a blended number against an organic goal.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Upside | Risk | Why rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small LinkedIn test | Faster meetings | Contaminates HubSpot original source | Goal is organic |
| Retargeting only | "Cheap" | Still paid; still contaminates | Same |

## Consequences

### Positive

- [[02-discoverability/search-and-answers]] has to actually work.
- Lena's calendar is not filled with the wrong plants.

### Negative and trade-offs

- Pipeline will be slow in Q3. That is accepted.
