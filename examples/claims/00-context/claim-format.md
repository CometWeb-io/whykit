# Optional claim format

This synthetic design note records the example's format choice. Keep source
records E-NNN separate from reviewed claim records C-NNN. The opt-in changes
configuration, not historical decisions or the meaning of source_ids.

A receipt binds local captured bytes and attribution; it does not authenticate
a reviewer or establish factual truth. An approved decision may accept a
reviewed conflict when its Claim assessment explains the trade-off.
