# Competitor: Vendor A (synthetic example)

> This fictional profile demonstrates how to record a competitor hypothesis. It
> does not describe or make claims about a real supplier.

## Research question

How can a team distinguish an enterprise reliability platform from a
shift-level workflow product without presenting assumptions as market facts?

## Source

- Title: Synthetic Vendor A product and pricing page
- Location: <https://vendor-a.example/product>
- Evidence-register ID: E-005
- The `.example` domain is reserved; this is teaching data, not a live source.

## Demonstration findings

- In this fictional scenario, Vendor A sells a reliability platform to an
  enterprise engineering team.
- Its sample product surface is an asset health dashboard, rather than a
  supervisor's shift list.
- Pricing is intentionally unspecified; the example does not invent a price.

## Interpretation

Within the Northline scenario, this fictional vendor represents a different
buyer and workflow. Use it to demonstrate how a decision can cite an evidence
record and state the limits of the comparison. See D-002 and
[[01-strategy/positioning]].

## Limitations

Every supplier detail above is fabricated for this example. Do not reuse it as
competitive intelligence or attribute it to a real company.
