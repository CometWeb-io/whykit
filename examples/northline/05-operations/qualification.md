# Qualification

An unmarked "lead" in HubSpot is not a fact about demand. These are the gates.

## MQL

A demo request from a work email. Nothing else. Page views are not MQLs.

## SQL

All of:

1. Buyer role is ops, maintenance, or plant management — not IT-only.
2. Plant is discrete manufacturing.
3. Headcount is in the ICP band, or a documented exception by Product Lead.
4. A demo happened, and the champion can name the machines that would go on the list.

Disqualified deals keep their reason. "Not a fit" is not a reason. Use: process-industry, IT-incumbent, no CMMS, too small, no champion.
