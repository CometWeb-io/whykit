# Competitor: Vendor B (synthetic example)

> This fictional profile demonstrates a product comparison. It does not describe
> or make claims about a real supplier.

## Research question

How should a team record a hypothetical competitor whose workflow appears closer
to its own product?

## Source

- Title: Synthetic Vendor B product page
- Location: <https://vendor-b.example/product>
- Evidence-register ID: E-006
- The `.example` domain is reserved; this is teaching data, not a live source.

## Demonstration findings

- In this fictional scenario, Vendor B uses wireless sensors for condition
  monitoring and targets maintenance teams.
- Its sample software surface is an asset dashboard, not a next-shift task list.
- The scenario suggests a possible workflow comparison, but supplies no real
  win/loss evidence.

## Interpretation

These fictional details let the example show how to distinguish an observed
source statement from a hypothesis about buyer choice. Any claim about a real
head-to-head would need independently collected evidence.

> [!hypothesis] Hypothesis
> If a prospect compares this fictional Vendor B with Pulse, workflow may matter
> more than detection. This is untested scenario text, not a market finding.

## Limitations

All supplier details and product claims are invented teaching material. Do not
reuse them as competitive intelligence or attribute them to a real company.
