# Offer architecture

## What is sold

1. **Pulse subscription** — per machine, annual. Includes the machine list, exception workflow, and CMMS export.
2. **Sensor kit** — hardware, sold, not rented. Installation is the customer's maintenance team, with a remote Pulse onboarding.
3. **Onboarding** — fixed scope, fixed duration, four-customer cap. E-011.

There is no professional-services SKU. If a deal requires one, it is the wrong deal.

## What is not sold

- Custom models per machine family as a line item. The product either supports the machine or it does not.
- A "platform licence" for IT. That is how we get pulled into a six-month security review we will lose to an incumbent.
- Process-industry packages. [[06-decisions/d-008-discrete|D-008]].

## Packaging

Public pricing is not published. Range is given on the sales call after qualification. Publishing a number is an open question, not a plan.

> [!recommendation] Recommendation
> Publish a starting price on the site once organic demo quality is stable for a quarter. Not before. A price on a page that attracts the wrong plant is an expensive filter we do not yet need.

## Delivery constraint

The offer is shaped by E-011. Four parallel onboardings. The commercial team does not discount its way around a full delivery queue; it waitlists.
