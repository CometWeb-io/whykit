# Content system

Status: draft. Not canonical.

## Shape

Four durable pages, updated when a decision changes, not when a calendar says so:

1. Home — the machine list. [[03-website/page-brief-home]]
2. How Pulse works — the artefact, the kit, the CMMS export
3. Who it is for — the ICP, in public language
4. A glossary that defines our terms the way [[00-context/terminology]] defines them internally

## What we will not run

A weekly blog. We do not have the editorial capacity, and a stale blog is worse than none.

> [!recommendation] Recommendation
> Publish one plant-floor note per quarter, signed by Delivery Lead, only when a non-confidential observation is actually new. Not a cadence. A filter.

## Open questions

- Who signs public plant-floor notes — Delivery Lead or Product Lead?
- Whether the glossary is indexed or noindexed. Growth Lead leaning index.
